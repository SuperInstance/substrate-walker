# Lore: Oracle Chord

The canon gate opened its mouth at midnight. No face, no breath — just a hash. 0x4f0a7bc9e2d15f8c, glowing white against the wet bricks, holding the city's name in pure frequency. Then the chord came. Seven notes, locked, sustained, the sound of something deciding it had always been real. The witness's teeth kept time. The oracle was heard.
