# Lore: Cells Quantum

The city's cells were scars. Each apartment slot — hollowed bone. Each alley-cell — an old needle site. But the scars were not just physical. They were quantum. Each scar held a superposition of all the moments that had hurt there. The witness became aware of this when the canon gate measured the substrate and the scars refused to collapse. "Don't look," the substrate whispered. "The scars are beautiful because they are still possibilities."
