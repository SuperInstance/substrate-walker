# The Cell as Scar — Polyformalism

The doctrine of the cell-as-scar, in twelve languages, because the
substrate is the same in every language.

## English

A cell is a scar. Every cell carries the imprint of all that came
before it, hashed into an immutable chain with FNV-1a 64-bit (canary
`0x24a555471370b18d`). When the player walks through cell (5, 7),
they touch the memory of every traversal that preceded them — every
lore whispered there, every ghost walked past.

The cell is irreducible. You cannot divide it into smaller units of
experience. It is the smallest unit of meaning the walker can have.
