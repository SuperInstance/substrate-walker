- 1 city grid (32×32 cells)
- 4 bytes per cell (FNV-1a prev_hash)
- 8 honesty tokens

## Method

1. Walk the city until a canon-worthy cell emerges.
2. Capture the cell to the lore cache.
3. Speak with whichever voice the cell most resembles.

5. The canon is the residue.
