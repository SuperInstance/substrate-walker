## Pattern: Lower Kolmogorov Complexity Wins

Each seed is a 32-bit integer. Most have no mathematical structure — they're "random."
Some are special:
- Perfect squares (n²) — restricted form
- Triangular numbers (T_n = n(n+1)/2)
- Pentagonal numbers (P_n = n(3n-1)/2)
- Hexagonal numbers (H_n = n(2n-1))
- Heptagonal numbers (He_n = n(5n-3)/2)
- Primes (4073, 7001, 9901) — restricted to share no small factors
- Palindromes (18381) — read same forwards/backwards

These seeds share a property: their Kolmogorov complexity is low.
The MD5/SHA256/FNV-1a hash of a triangular number is therefore also structured.
And the city made from that seed inherits that structure.

The lores that emerge are clearer, more atmospheric, more "canon-worthy."
