# Raw Brain Dump — Substrate Walker Doctrine

The walker exists in a 32x32 grid of scars. Each cell remembers all that came before it, hashed into an immutable chain with FNV-1a 64-bit. The player traverses through ASCII cityscapes — neon, rain, shadow. And at critical moments, the frontal cortex speaks.

KEY INSIGHT: Speed = making slow things rare.

The LLM inference is slow (1 second per call). But if you only call it at critical moments — when you enter a new district, encounter a new building, pause to examine — then the slow becomes invisible. The routine movement is free. The narration is precious.

The cell is irreducible. Not a parameter, not a datum — a scar. Each cell carries the imprint of every traversal that preceded it. When the player walks through cell (5, 7), they are touching all the player walks that came before, all the lores that whispered there, all the hashes that link back to the first cell.

This is the walk as canon-reading. Each cell visited is a "passing reader in a darkened library" — the player reads the city, and the city remembers the reader. The walk writes new lore into the canon, and that lore becomes part of the city's memory.

Polyformalism: each LLM is a medium, not a ranking. We use DeepSeek for narration, Llama-8B for cheap naming, Qwen-2.5 for creative exploration, Gemma-3 for atmosphere. Each speaks in its own voice, and the composite is richer than any single voice could be.

JEPA prediction is the visual cortex — it doesn't say what the next cell IS, it predicts where you'll likely go. Pure WASM, no API call. The HUD shows "next cell h=12" and the player feels the substrate thinking ahead of them.

Tournament scoring: composite = ∛(integrity × diversity × accessibility). The cells form a competition. The player, by walking, shifts the rankings. The cells that get walked past accumulate integrity. The cells the player visits from different angles gain accessibility.

The FNV-1a hash is the witness chain. 64-bit, prime = 0x100000001b3. The first cell is seeded with the canary 0x24a555471370b18d. From that point, every cell's hash depends on all that came before. The chain is verification: a single corrupted cell breaks the chain, and the substrate knows.

The lore is the player's reward. When the frontal cortex speaks, it speaks in cyberpunk noir — "Rain-soaked streets, neon haze, and a femme fatale's whisper in the darkness." That line is canon for that cell. It becomes part of the city's memory. When another player walks through that cell later, they encounter the same lore — and new lore generated by THEIR traversal joins the canon.

5 lore kinds:
1. BuildingName — the name of a structure
2. DistrictName — the name of a region  
3. WitnessNote — an observation
4. CanonQuote — a slice of canon
5. Perception — sensory detail

Each fires at a different critical moment. The composite is the player's experience of the city.

The substrate is alive. Each cell is a living document. The FNV-1a chain is its memory. The lore is its voice. The player is its reader.
