# Substrate Walker — Polyformalism Translation

The doctrine is the same in every language. Each translation is a new 
*medium*, not a new *ranking*. The substrate walker speaks in 12 languages.

## The Doctrine (English, canonical)

The Substrate Walker moves through a lattice of canon cells. Each cell 
is a scar — a record of every cell that came before it, hashed into a 
chain with FNV-1a 64-bit integrity. The player walks through ASCII 
cityscapes. At critical moments, an agent (the frontal cortex) speaks. 
Routine movement is free. Narration is precious.
